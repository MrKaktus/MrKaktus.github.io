**Shoulder pitch/flexion gearmotor** *2025- (Ubuntu)* ![](./Shoulders.png width=50%) Assembling shoulder pitch/flexion gearmotor (gearbox and motor). It is used by M1 and M2 motors in left and right arm, and its assembly is identical for both. ## Gearbox Parts: - 1x M6C12 gearbox housing front (3d printed) - [M6C12_Gearbox_housing_front.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Gearbox_housing_front.stl) - [M6C12_Gearbox_housing_front.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Gearbox_housing_front.3mf) - 1x Shoulder gearbox housing middle (3d printed) - [M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_middle.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_middle.stl) - [M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_middle.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_middle.3mf) - (Original name: obj_2_6512_Housing_Base_Housing_Middle) - 1x Shoulder gearbox housing back (3d printed) - [M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_back.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_back.stl) - [M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_back.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Shoulder_gearbox_housing_back.3mf) - (Original name: obj_1_6512_Housing_Base_Housing_Front) - 2x 55 x 72 x 9mm ball bearing (for securing geartrain in housing) - [6811ZZ ball bearing 55mm ID 72mm OD 9mm Bore](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B082PW8F3S?th=1) - 1x M6C12 geartrain [(previously assembled)](./../2026_03_20_M6C12_Geartrain_assembly/2026_03_20_M6C12_Geartrain_assembly.html) Shoulder gearbox housing back looks to be originally designed as a front part, yet its located at the back of the gearbox (on a side where motor housing gets attached), thus I've renamed it in my project to correctly reflect that. ![All the parts before assembly.](./Gearmotor_1_800.jpg) I start by inserting ball bearing into housing front part. Then I insert geartrain into it (from output shaft front side). By now, used geartrain should have properly aligned gears. This allows me to easily slide whole stack into gearbox housing. ![Geartrain inserted into shoulder gearbox housing middle.](./Gearmotor_2_800.jpg) I can then secure whole geartrain with second ball bearing, and close gearbox with its back part. ![Secured geartrain with both ball bearings.](./Gearmotor_3_800.jpg) ![Complete shoulder gearbox (side, back view).](./Gearmotor_4_800.jpg) ![Complete shoulder gearbox (top, back view).](./Gearmotor_5_800.jpg) ## Gearmotor Parts: - 1x M6C12 motor housing [(previously assembled)](./../2026_03_13_M6C12_Motor_housing_assembly/2026_03_13_M6C12_Motor_housing_assembly.html) - 1x M6C12 shoulder pitch/flexion gearbox (assembled above) - 8x M3 x 0.5 x 60mm screws (for connecting gearbox and motor housing) - [Partially Threaded Alloy Steel 12.9 Grade Hex Socket Head Cap Screws](https://a.co/d/03GjSVTq) This is another place where original project is wrong. Screws are 60mm instead of 55mm, as those are too short to connect both parts. ![All the parts before assembly.](./Gearmotor_6_800.jpg) Assembling final gearmotor requires "key" bump on 3d printed motor shaft stiffener to match keyhole in 3d printed input motor shaft. We also expect hext standoff to fit snugly into that input motor shaft. Everything needs to align and fit perfectly, otherwise it won't work. ![Keyhole in input shaft.](./Gearmotor_7_800.jpg) As you can see it is very thight space once both parts are placed on joining them screws. I try to align key bump as much as possible ahead of time, and then once I push motor housing towards gearbox housing, I delicately turn motor rotor to get final fit. Once that happens motor shaft and hex standoff should finally click into place and motor housing should fully slide next to gearbox leaving no gap between the two. ![Key extrusion on motor shaft side.](./Gearmotor_8_800.jpg) ![Final assembly.](./Gearmotor_9_800.jpg)
