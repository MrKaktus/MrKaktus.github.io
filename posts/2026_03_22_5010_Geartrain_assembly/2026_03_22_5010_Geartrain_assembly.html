**5010 geartrain** *2025- (Ubuntu)* Most of the steps for assembling 5010 reduction gearbox geartrain are the same as in M6C12 geartrain assembly case, with few small differences. Thus its best to open both pages in side-by-side split view. ## Gears Parts: - 2x 5010 gear (3d printed) - [5010_Gear.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/5010_Gear.stl) - [5010_Gear.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/5010_Gear.3mf) - (Original name: 5010_Cycloidal_Disk) - 2x 12 x 18 x 4mm ball bearing (for placing gears on input shaft) - [6701-2RS ball bearing 12mm ID 18mm OD 4mm Bore](https://a.co/d/02LF4ujl) - 1x 5010 input shaft (3d printed) - [5010_Input_shaft.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/5010_Input_shaft.stl) - [5010_Input_shaft.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/5010_Input_shaft.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Input_Shaft) Process of assembling gears on input shaft is the same as in case of M6C12 geartrain. ## Output shaft front Parts: - 1x 5010 output shaft front (3d printed) - [5010_Output_shaft_front.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/5010_Output_shaft_front.stl) - [5010_Output_shaft_front.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/5010_Output_shaft_front.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Output_Shaft_Front) - 6x M3 Threaded inserts (attachment points to actuator): - [M3 x 4 x 5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0BTYF2MMD) - 13x 6 x 10 x 3mm ball bearing (placed between output shaft and gears) - [MR106-2RS ball bearing 6mm ID 10mm OD 3mm Bore](https://a.co/d/08xgLrtE) - 1x Spacers, set of six (3d printed) - [Output_shaft_spacers.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/Output_shaft_spacers.stl) - [Output_shaft_spacers.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/Output_shaft_spacers.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Cycloidal_Actuator_Spacers) While I've spent a lot of time sanding off M6C12 geartrain output shaft fronts to fit ball bearings onto them, in case of 5010 ones, everything just fit right from the start. This saved me a lot of time. Its possible that overextrudnig correction did a trick here, as I had that figured out by then. ![Assembled output shaft fronts of motors 13 and 14.](./5010_geartrain_1_800.jpg) ## Output shaft back Parts: - 1x 5010 output shaft back (3d printed) - [5010_Output_shaft_back.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/5010_Output_shaft_back.stl) - [5010_Output_shaft_back.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/5010_Output_shaft_back.3mf) - (Original name: 5010_Output_Shaft_Rear) - 6x M3 Threaded inserts (for attaching output shaft back to the front): - [M3 x 4 x 5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0BTYF2MMD) ![Output shaft back with threaded inserts in place.](./5010_geartrain_4_800.jpg) ## Geartrain Parts: - 6x M3 x 0.5 x 25mm screws (for connecting front and back of output shaft) - [12.9 Grade Alloy Steel Metric Machine Hex Socket Screw](https://a.co/d/00OqtjJo) ![All the parts before assembly.](./5010_geartrain_2_800.jpg) Notice that screws are now 25mm instead of 30mm (as it was in case of M6C12 motor geartrain). Remember to correctly align gears, so that later whole geartrain fits into the gearbox housing. ![Complete output shaft with gears.](./5010_geartrain_5_800.jpg)
