**Hot inserting** *2025- (Ubuntu)* While on first glance it looks like pretty straight forward thing to do, there are few caveats to properly performing hot inserting of brass nuts into PLA fillament. Warming up Fist of all, by default iron warms up to its max (in case of the one I bought its 350 degrees celcius). This is definitely too hot for PLA. I found 220-230 degrees temperature range to be best for this. You can also try 180-200 degrees temperature range if you want to be extra cautious. I've also noticed that iron will very quickly report that its tip reached given temperature, while its not true (probably due to placement of its temperature sensor). Thus I always give it a moment to properly warm up. Tip too cold If tip is too cold, nut will start to go in under applied pressure, but the plastic around it will cave in too, creating what looks like a crater. TODO: Attach photo. Tip too hot If the tip is too warm, plastic will be pushing nut out (giving impression of bouncing back), and will go outside on its own too, bulging around. In such case plastic around nuts looses its structural integrity becoming semi-liquid. It's easy in such case for a mistake like skewed nut (see hint below). !!! Hint Hint Always have one screw ready when hot inserting nuts. If your hand slips and nut gets tilted, there is still a way to fix it. You will need to slightly drive in screw from the other side (or if impossible just push it till it touches nut). Then hold nut between that screw and hot iron, and move the surrounding plastic part to correct nut orientation relative to it. Screw should then be able to slide into the nut, and few turns should be able to be made. This can avoid few hours of reprinting the part, but in general its of course better to just use correct temperature. TL/DR In general, with properly warmed up iron, it boils down to patience. Don't apply too much pressure, just slight one. Let plastic melt on its own and slowly absorb the nut. ![Hot inserting process.](./../2026_03_20_M6C12_Geartrain_assembly/Output_shaft_front_3.MOV)
