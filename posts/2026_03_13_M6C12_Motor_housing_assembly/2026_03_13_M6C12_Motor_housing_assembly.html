**M6C12 motor housing** *2025- (Ubuntu)* Assembling M6C12 motor housing and motor shaft. Most of the steps are the same as in 5010 housing assembly case, with few small differences. Thus its best to open both pages in side-by-side split view. ## Housing Parts: - 1x Motor shell (3d printed) - [M6C12_Motor_shell_v1_1.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Motor_shell_v1_1.stl) - [M6C12_Motor_shell_v1_1.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Motor_shell_v1_1.3mf) - 8x M3 Threaded inserts (for attaching to gearbox housing): - [M3 x 4 x 5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0BTYF2MMD) - 4x M2 Threaded inserts (for mounting rotary encoder): - [M2 x 3 x 3.5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B8GN63S2) In original Berkeley Humanoid Lite project, M6C12 motor housing CAD is invalid. Holes for mounting specified rotary encoder are spaced too far apart, making final assembly of M6C12 actuator impossible. You can see this issue in original assembly video too. Unfortunately I've discovered this after I've 3d printed motor housings for all M6C12 motors in advance. This is lesson in itself - to always do initial assembly on one gearbox, gearmotor, actuator, etc. before mass producing all remaining parts of given type. ![Original M6C12 motor shell model with invalid mounting points.](./M6C12_housing_1_800.jpg) There is another issue with original design. Motor is mounted in a way, that power supply cables are routed through the side of the motor housing. That is fine for 5010 motor, as it has different shape, but for M6C12 motor is a serious issue, because once its placed in that housing, its pushing those power cables slightly up, causing them to collide with rotor. ![Power supply cables dangerously close to rotor.](./M6C12_housing_2_800.jpg) As I needed to re-print those housings, I've decided to fix above issue along the way. I've rotated all internal holes by 45 degrees. This changed the way power supply cables are routed from the motor. Instead of sticking out on a side, they now bend up to the back of the housing, in direction of rotary encoder and controller board. This way they no longer collide with rotor, and can be fully enclosed in final actuator housing. We couldn't do it in case of 5010 housing as its too small, but fortunately it is not an issue here. ![Parts placement after re-designing motor housing.](./M6C12_housing_3_800.jpg) Links to motor shell part are already leading to re-designed part which I indicated with 1.1 version. Notice also that M6C12 has eight M3 threaded inserts instead of four (as it was in case of 5010 housing). ![Final M3 and M2 threaded inserts locations afer hot inserting.](./M6C12_housing_4_800.jpg) ## Rotary encoder magnet Parts: - 1x [MAD M6C12 EEE 150KV Brushless Motor](https://mad-motor.com/products/mad-components-m6c12-eee-industrial-drone-motor?VariantsId=10508) - 1x Magnet (sourced from rotary encoder package) Process of attaching rotary encoder magnet on top of motor shaft is the same as in case of 5010 motor. ## Housing and motor Parts: - 4x M3 x 8mm screws (sourced from M6C12 motor box) ![M6C12 motor secured inside of housing with M3x8mm screws.](./M6C12_housing_5_800.jpg) ## Motor shaft Parts: - 1x Motor shaft stiffener (3d printed) - [M6C12_Motor_shaft.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Motor_shaft.stl) - [M6C12_Motor_shaft.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Motor_shaft.3mf) - 4x [M3 x 0.5 x 10mm screws](https://a.co/d/0gWb3teZ) - 1x [M4 x 15mm Female Threaded Hex Standoff Pillar](https://a.co/d/0b5IcD6g) Tools: - 1x [4mm Metric M35 Cobalt Steel Twist Drill Bit](https://a.co/d/0bUsTGEk) Notice that hex standoff is now 15mm instead of 12mm (as it was in case of 5010 motor shaft). ![Final assembly.](./M6C12_housing_7_800.jpg)
