**M6C12 geartrain** *2025- (Ubuntu)* Assembling geartrain is the next step after motor housing. It is crucial part of actuator just next to motor itself. Its role is to reduce motor rotation speed, increasing actuator torque. Below I'm describing process of assembling M6C12 reduction gearbox geartrain. Thats the place where I've encountered almost all the precision issues. Input shaft, gears, output shaft - all were affected. I've described them together with mitigations I took in [Precision issues](./../2026_02_27_Precision_issues/2026_02_27_Precision_issues.html) section. ## Gears Parts: - 2x M6C12 gear (3d printed) - [M6C12_Gear.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Gear.stl) - [M6C12_Gear.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Gear.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Cycloidal_Actuator_Cycloidal+Disk) - 2x 12 x 18 x 4mm ball bearing (for placing gears on input shaft) - [6701-2RS ball bearing 12mm ID 18mm OD 4mm Bore](https://a.co/d/02LF4ujl) - 1x M6C12 input shaft (3d printed) - [M6C12_Input_shaft.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Input_shaft.stl) - [M6C12_Input_shaft.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Input_shaft.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Input_Shaft) Gears assembly with input shaft is pretty straightforward and can be documented with few photos: ![All the parts before assembly.](./Gears_1_800.jpg) ![6701-2RS ball bearings inserted into gears.](./Gears_2_800.jpg) ![Input shaft inserted into first gear bearing.](./Gears_3_800.jpg) ![Second gear inserted on top of input shaft.](./Gears_4_800.jpg) ![Gears and input shaft assembled together.](./Gears_5_800.jpg) Notice how small is the gap between both gears. They need to remain perfectly perpendicular to input shaft axis. I'm sceptical of this holding up during regular actuator use. I feel that current parts are too small to be made out of PLA and that they will get tilted/warped easily as this fillament is too soft for this kind of use. I might be wrong of course. I assume the only way to know is to have those actuators in use for prolonged period of time. ## Output shaft front Parts: - 1x M6C12 output shaft front (3d printed) - [M6C12_Output_shaft_front.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Output_shaft_front.stl) - [M6C12_Output_shaft_front.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Output_shaft_front.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Output_Shaft_Front) - 6x M3 Threaded inserts (attachment points to actuator): - [M3 x 4 x 5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0BTYF2MMD) - 13x 6 x 10 x 3mm ball bearing (placed between output shaft and gears) - [MR106-2RS ball bearing 6mm ID 10mm OD 3mm Bore](https://a.co/d/08xgLrtE) - 1x Spacers, set of six (3d printed) - [Output_shaft_spacers.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/Output_shaft_spacers.stl) - [Output_shaft_spacers.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/Output_shaft_spacers.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Cycloidal_Actuator_Spacers) ![All the parts before assembly.](./Output_shaft_front_1_800.jpg) ![Placement of threaded nuts before hot inserting.](./Output_shaft_front_2_800.jpg) ![Hot inserting process.](./Output_shaft_front_3.MOV) There is more to hot inserting process than it seems. I've described all the details in [Hot inserting](./../2026_02_28_Hot_inserting/2026_02_28_Hot_inserting.html) section. ![First layer of ball bearings inserted on output shaft columns.](./Output_shaft_front_5_800.jpg) As described before, I had plenty of precision issues with all the parts composing geartrain. In case of output shaft front, its six columns were too thick to insert MR106-2RS ball bearings onto them. Notice there is also one extra bearing inserted in the center. Its there to support end of input shaft. ![Bearing spacers inserted on top of MR106-2RS ball bearings.](./Output_shaft_front_6_800.jpg) ![Output shaft front assembled with second layer of bearings on top.](./Output_shaft_front_8_800.jpg) ## Output shaft back Parts: - 1x M6C12 output shaft back (3d printed) - [M6C12_Output_shaft_back.stl](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/stl/M6C12_Output_shaft_back.stl) - [M6C12_Output_shaft_back.3mf](./../../projects/2025_Humanoid/data/3mf/M6C12_Output_shaft_back.3mf) - (Original name: 6512_Output_Shaft_Rear) - 6x M3 Threaded inserts (for attaching output shaft back to the front): - [M3 x 4 x 5mm Threaded Insert Knurled Brass Nuts Female Thread Knurled](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0BTYF2MMD) Output shaft back needs to get 6 threaded nuts hot inserted as well. Those will be attachment points for screws connecting both parts together. ![Output shaft back (left) and front (right) with threaded inserts in place.](./Output_shaft_back_800.jpg) ## Geartrain Parts: - 6x M3 x 0.5 x 30mm screws (for connecting front and back of output shaft) - [12.9 Grade Alloy Steel Metric Machine Hex Socket Screw](https://a.co/d/015ZhZix) ![All the parts before assembly.](./Output_shaft_1_800.jpg) ![Gears inserted onto output shaft front.](./Output_shaft_2_800.jpg) Notice input shaft direction - its end is placed in the central ball bearing. There is also one more thing that needs to be done before closing output shaft and final assembly. We need to ensure, that both gears are properly placed relative to each other. To do that I've 3d printed M6C12 gearbox housing that normally is not used in robot assembly (you can also use any of the gearbox housing parts used for gearmotor assemblies). Simply try to slide it from the top onto both gears. See example below: ![Incorrectly aligned gears.](./Output_shaft_4_800.jpg) When gears are incorrectly aligned, like in photo above, second gear will collide with housing teeth, and thus prevent geartrain from fully sliding into it. In such case, you will need to remove gears from output shaft front, and rotate one of them relative to other into alternate position. All six holes in gears need to overlap each other in identical way, and allow reinsertion onto output shaft front. Once you do that, try sliding gearbox housing on top of it again. Unfortunately I haven't found recipie on how to reliably align those gears in one go, so its a process of trial and error until you find proper position (as seen below). ![Correctly aligned gears fit into gearbox housing.](./Output_shaft_5_800.jpg) Once the proper alignment is done, output shaft back can be placed on top of output shaft front for final assembly. ![Output shaft ready for assembly (back).](./Output_shaft_6_800.jpg) ![Output shaft ready for assembly (front).](./Output_shaft_7_800.jpg) ![Screws inserted from the front.](./Output_shaft_8_800.jpg) While connecting 3d printed parts together using screws, its important to remember to not overtighten them. When several screws need to be used, I first drive them all in, leaving them slightly loose. Then, I tighten them all down, working in pairs of screws located on opposite sides to distribute any tension evenly. ![Complete geartrain.](./Output_shaft_9_800.jpg)
